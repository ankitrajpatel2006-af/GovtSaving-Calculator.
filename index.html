FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about GovtSave calculators, savings estimates and scheme information.

Are GovtSave calculator results official?
No. GovtSave provides educational estimates only. Actual interest, maturity amounts, eligibility, tax treatment and scheme rules may differ. Always verify current information with the relevant official authority or financial institution.
Which savings schemes can I calculate?
GovtSave currently provides calculators for SSY, PPF, POMIS, NPS and SCSS. The available calculators use the assumptions and inputs shown on each calculator.
Are the rates shown on the website guaranteed?
No. Rates displayed on GovtSave are reference assumptions configured for the calculators. Government and financial institution rates can change, so verify the latest rate before making an investment decision.
Can I download my calculation as a PDF?
Yes. The existing savings calculators include a PDF report option after a calculation has been generated.
Does GovtSave store the values I enter?
The calculators and PDF reports run in your browser. GovtSave's current privacy information states that calculator values are not sent to a GovtSave server. Avoid entering sensitive information such as Aadhaar, PAN or account numbers.
Is GovtSave financial advice?
No. GovtSave is an independent educational calculator platform. Calculator results are estimates and should not be treated as personalised financial advice.
