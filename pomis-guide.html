Post Office MIS: Monthly Income Explained

Account limits, term, payout and premature-closure basics for the Post Office Monthly Income Scheme.

What is the Post Office Monthly Income Scheme?

POMIS is a 5-year post office deposit scheme that pays interest every month, making it useful for people who want a predictable monthly income while keeping their principal safe. The principal is returned at maturity.

Key facts at a glance

  • Interest rate used in our calculator: p.a., simple interest paid monthly
  • Single account limit: ₹9,00,000 · Joint account limit: ₹15,00,000
  • Term: 5 years · Minimum deposit ₹1,000 in multiples of ₹1,000

How monthly income is estimated

Monthly income = deposit × annual rate ÷ 12. For example, a ₹3,00,000 deposit at the configured rate gives the amount shown in our POMIS calculator every month, and the same amount multiplied by 60 is the total interest over five years.

Single vs joint accounts

  • A single account is held by one adult (or a guardian on behalf of a minor or person of unsound mind).
  • A joint account can be held by up to three adults, with a higher combined limit.
  • Investments in a joint account are shared equally by the holders for the purposes of the limits.

Premature closure

Closure is generally not allowed in the first year. After that, a small deduction from the principal usually applies, and the deduction is lower if you close later. Check the current schedule with your post office.

Tax

Interest is taxable according to your income-tax slab. The deposit does not qualify for a Section 80C deduction. Verify the latest treatment before investing.

Official sources
