Senior Citizens Savings Scheme (SCSS) Explained

Eligibility, deposit limits, quarterly interest and premature-closure rules for the Senior Citizens Savings Scheme.

What is the Senior Citizens Savings Scheme?

SCSS is a government-backed small savings scheme designed for senior citizens who want regular, predictable income. Interest is paid quarterly, and the deposit is returned at the end of the term. It replaces the older Mahila Samman Savings Certificate calculator on GovtSave, because MSSC is no longer open to new investors.

Key facts at a glance

  • Interest rate used in our calculator: p.a., paid quarterly
  • Minimum deposit ₹1,000 · Maximum deposit ₹30,00,000 (in multiples of ₹1,000)
  • Tenure: 5 years, with an option to extend once for 3 years, subject to rules

Who is eligible?

  • Individuals aged 60 years or above.
  • Retirees between 55 and 60 who opened the account within the prescribed time after receiving retirement benefits, subject to conditions.
  • Defence-service retirees aged 50 or above, subject to conditions.
  • Accounts can be opened singly or jointly with a spouse.

How quarterly interest is estimated

Quarterly interest = deposit × annual rate ÷ 4. Over the 5-year term there are 20 payouts, so the total interest is the quarterly amount multiplied by 20. Because interest is paid out and not compounded, the principal stays the same until maturity.

Premature closure

An account can usually be closed after one year, but a deduction applies, and the amount depends on how long the account has been open. Check the current schedule before deciding.

Tax

The deposit may qualify for a Section 80C deduction under the old tax regime, while the interest is taxable. TDS can apply when interest crosses the prescribed threshold. Because thresholds change, verify the current rule and consider submitting the relevant declaration if you are eligible.
