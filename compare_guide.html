PPF vs SSY vs SCSS vs FD: Understanding the Differences

An educational side-by-side look at eligibility, term, payout and tax for popular savings options.

Why compare by rules, not just rates?

A higher headline rate is not automatically a better choice. Savings products differ in who can invest, how long the money is locked in, how and when returns are paid, and how they are taxed. Compare the features that matter for your goal.

FeatureSSY · PPF · SCSS · Bank FD
Rate used on GovtSaveSSY · PPF · SCSS · FD (illustrative)
Who can investSSY: girl child accounts · PPF: resident individuals · SCSS: senior citizens · FD: most adults
TermSSY 21 years · PPF 15 years (extendable) · SCSS 5 years · FD chosen by you
Return payoutSSY and PPF: lump sum at maturity · SCSS: quarterly · FD: depends on option
TaxSSY and PPF are generally exempt on interest and maturity; SCSS and FD interest is generally taxable. Verify the current rules.

How to compare responsibly

  • Use the same investment amount and time horizon where you can.
  • Separate notified, fixed-rate products from market-linked ones like NPS.
  • Check lock-in, premature-withdrawal rules and how quickly you can access your money.
  • Look at your own tax situation, not just general statements.
