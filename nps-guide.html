NPS guide

NPS Calculator: Estimate Your Retirement Corpus

Understand how monthly contributions, time and assumed returns shape a projected NPS corpus.

What is the National Pension System?

The NPS is a voluntary, market-linked retirement savings system regulated by PFRDA. Your contributions are invested across asset classes such as equity, corporate bonds and government securities, and the value depends on market performance.

Returns are not guaranteed

NPS returns vary. That is why our calculator lets you choose 6%, 8%, 10% or 12% as a hypothetical annual return. Try several values and plan with the lower ones in mind.

How the calculator works

  1. You enter your monthly contribution and current age.
  2. The calculator counts the months from your age to 60, the standard retirement age.
  3. Each month the balance grows at the monthly equivalent of your chosen return and your contribution is added at month-end.
  4. The result is the estimated corpus, split into your contributions and the estimated growth.

What is not included

  • Fund management and other scheme charges
  • Income tax on withdrawals or annuity income
  • Annuity pricing and the required annuity purchase at exit
  • Changes in your contribution over time (for example, yearly increases)

Things to check before investing

  • Current rules on lump-sum withdrawal and the mandatory annuity share at exit.
  • Tax benefits available under the tax regime you follow.
  • Your asset allocation choice (active or auto) and the fund manager's track record.
